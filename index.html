About

I am an emerging writer-director drawn to queer Irish working-class stories that blend humour and unease while interrogating cultural identity and pride. Fresh off my questionable film degree, I was audibly relieved to have my debut comedy script selected for a BBC X ARTS COUNCIL ENGLAND filmmaking program. HOLD THE SAUSAGE 2021 aired on BBC Four and is available to watch on iPlayer — when you have five minutes — or like now?

My SOMETHING YOU CANNOT NAME 2022 gave me the excuse to record myself dancing atop the Navan Fort, a sacral mound leading ‘to other worlds’ in my hometown. Ignoring local ‘lost the plot’ rumours, I was able to blend the results with archived footage as a participant of the BBC 100: MAKING FILM HISTORY PROJECT to visually experiment with themes of dual Irish nationality.

My collaborator-and-crowdfunder powered raunchy comedy ASL 2024 graced many festivals including BAFTA-qualifying SQIFF 2025! I am developing my gays and girls driven comedy horror DEAD ON with Irish-led London production company AnyOther - PS it’s fucking class. Since my wit is yet to pay my bills, I freelance as a PA for commercials like SharkNinja, Burger King & Tesco. What can’t I do?
