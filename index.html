About

Cal is an award-winning emerging film writer-director from Armagh.

Cal's work blends humour with unease to interrogate Irish culture, identity and pride.

Cal's work has been screened at multiple US & UK festivals including BAFTA-qualifying SQIFF.

Cal is developing his next comedy short DEAD ON and comedy TV series SKITTERS.

Cal is seeking literary representation (unless you find him first!) while already production-represented at CallBox.
