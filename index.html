About

Cal is an award-winning film writer-director drawn to stories that use humour in exploring Irish culture, queer identities and pride.

His debut comedy HOLD THE SAUSAGE was selected for the BBC X ARTS COUNCIL ENGLAND filmmaking program and is available to watch on iPlayer — when you have five minutes — or like now? SOMETHING YOU CANNOT NAME is an experimental film (with a choreographed cameo) made for BBC 100: MAKING FILM HISTORY PROJECT that explores dual Irish nationality. ASL is a collaborator and crowdfunded raunchy comedy that graced many festivals including BAFTA-qualifying SQIFF 2025!

Cal is developing his gays-and-girls driven comedy horror DEAD ON with Irish-led London production company ANYOTHER. Since Cal’s wit is yet to pay his bills, he freelances as a PA for commercials like SharkNinja, Burger King & Tesco. What can’t he do?
