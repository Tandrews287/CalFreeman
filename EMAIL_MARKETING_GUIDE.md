# 📧 Email Marketing Guide for Cal Freeman

Welcome to your email marketing setup! This guide explains in simple terms how everything works, how to log in, how to see everyone who signs up on your website, and how to send them newsletter updates.

---

## 1. How It Works (The Automatic Setup)

Everything is connected and runs completely on autopilot:

1. A visitor comes to **calfreeman.com**.
2. They scroll down to the **"Keep up with Cal"** box and type in their email address.
3. Two things happen instantly:
   * **Added to your List:** Their email is automatically saved into your Brevo contact list.
   * **You Get Notified:** Brevo immediately sends an email alert to **hello@calfreeman.com** saying: *"New Subscriber: user@example.com"*, so you always know when someone joins.

## 3. How to View Your List of Contacts

To see the list of people who have subscribed to your emails:

1. Log in to **Brevo**.
2. In the left-hand menu, click **CRM** -> **Contacts**.
3. Click **Lists** (or click directly on **Contacts** to see every individual email address).
4. Click on your list (named **Your first list** or **Marketing Contacts**).
5. Here, you can see:
   * Every subscriber's email address.
   * The date and time they signed up.
   * Total number of subscribers.

*(Note: You can also export this list as an Excel/CSV spreadsheet anytime by clicking **Export**).*

## 4. How to Send an Email to Everyone on Your List

Whenever you have news, festival screening announcements, new film releases, or updates, you can send an email blast to everyone with just a few clicks:

### Step 1: Create the Email
1. In the left menu, click **Campaigns** → **Email**.
2. Click the blue **Create an email campaign** button in the top right corner.
3. Give your campaign a name (e.g. *"Hold the Sausage Screening Announcement"* — this is only visible to you).

### Step 2: Fill in the 4 Steps
Brevo gives you a simple checklist:
* **From:** Already set to `calfreeman <hello@calfreeman.com>`.
* **To:** Click **Add recipients** and select your subscriber list.
* **Subject:** Write what subscribers will see in their inbox (e.g. *"New Screening Announcement from Cal Freeman"*).
* **Design:** Click **Design email**.
  * Pick the **Drag & Drop Editor** (or choose a simple template).
  * You can type your text, add photos/film stills, and insert links to trailers, tickets, or BBC iPlayer.

### Step 3: Test and Send
1. Click **Send a test** at the top right to send a preview to `hello@calfreeman.com` so you can review how it looks on your phone first.
2. When you're happy with it, click **Schedule** in the top right:
   * Choose **Send immediately** to blast it out right now, or
   * Pick a future date and time to schedule it.

## 5. Helpful Things to Know

* **Free Plan Limits:** Your Brevo account allows **unlimited contacts** for free, and you can send up to **300 emails per day** for free (~9,000 emails/month).
* **Legal Compliance (GDPR):** Brevo automatically attaches an "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of every marketing campaign, so you never have to worry about marketing email regulations.
* **Open Rates & Clicks:** After sending an email, you can click on **Campaigns** to see how many people opened your email and clicked your links!
